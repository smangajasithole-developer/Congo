ABOUT

The Story Behind The Sound

Born in Skumane and raised in Kamhlushwa, Congo discovered a passion for Deep House, Commercial, and Hip-Hop music at the age of 17. What started as bedroom mixing sessions quickly evolved into a full-blown career that has taken him to stages across four provinces.

Known for seamlessly blending Hip-Hop and Amapiano into unforgettable sonic journeys, Congo has become a sought-after name at Nabalabantu and VBF in Secunda. From intimate underground venues to massive festival stages, every set is crafted to create a unique connection with the audience.

Beyond performing, Congo is also a dedicated music producer with releases on top labels. His tracks have been supported by industry heavyweights and have accumulated millions of streams worldwide.
